# Data Center PUE and Cooling Optimization During Power Spikes

## Power Usage Effectiveness (PUE)
Power Usage Effectiveness (PUE) measures the ratio of total facility power to the IT equipment power consumption. Modern enterprise facilities target a PUE between 1.15 and 1.30.

## Thermal Surges and Mitigations
1. **Power Spike Correlation**: Abnormal thermal spikes are directly correlated with IT power surges (such as runaway loop processes or stuck hardware jobs).
2. **Cooling Overhead Escalation**: When IT loads spike abruptly, mechanical chillers ramp up disproportionately, raising the instantaneous PUE by up to 25%.
3. **Remediation Protocol**: Immediate investigation and remediation of operational anomalies (z-score >= 2.5) preserves cooling plant stability and prevents localized thermal hotspots.
