# GHG Protocol Scope 2 Location-Based Emissions Accounting

## Standards and Principles
Under the Greenhouse Gas Protocol Corporate Standard, Scope 2 emissions encompass indirect greenhouse gas emissions from the generation of purchased electricity, steam, heating, and cooling consumed by the reporting entity.

## Location-Based Method
1. **Grid Average Emission Factors**: The location-based method calculates electricity emissions using regional grid average emission factors (such as US EPA eGRID subregional rates or national grid averages).
2. **Formula**: $\text{Emissions } (\text{kgCO}_2\text{e}) = \text{Electricity Consumption } (\text{kWh}) \times \text{Regional Grid Factor } (\text{kgCO}_2\text{e/kWh})$.
3. **Data Quality and Uncertainty**: Factors must state their geographical boundaries, publication date, and greenhouse gas coverage (CO2, CH4, N2O converted to CO2-equivalent).
